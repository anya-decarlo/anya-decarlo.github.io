Measure definitions

How every number on this page is defined and where it comes from.

MeasureDefinitionSource fields
NT filingsCount of NT 10-K and NT 10-Q notices filed in the report week (Monday–Sunday, by EDGAR filing date) that match the filters. Amendments (NT 10-K/A) are excluded.EDGAR full-text search: form, file_date
Week-over-week changeNT filings in the report week minus the prior week, and the same week one year earlier (52 weeks back).Derived
Extension deadlineRule 12b-25 grants 15 calendar days for a 10-K and 5 for a 10-Q. Because the notice is due within one business day of the original deadline, the deadline is estimated as the NT filing date plus 15 or 5 days.file_date, form
Follow-through statusFiled in extension: the same CIK filed a 10-K/10-KT (or 10-Q/10-QT) on or before the extension deadline. Filed after extension: filed later, within a year. Refresh schedule and lineage

Every Monday at 11:00 UTC a GitHub Actions workflow (.github/workflows/refresh-late-filers.yml) runs refresh.py. The script pulls the trailing two years of NT 10-K/10-Q filings from EDGAR full-text search, then reads EDGAR's quarterly form indexes to match each notice to the company's next 10-K or 10-Q. It writes data.json and commits it, and GitHub Pages republishes this page. The workflow can also run on demand. Requests identify themselves with a User-Agent and stay under the SEC's 10-requests-per-second fair-access limit.
