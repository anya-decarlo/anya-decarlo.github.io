Variance Analysis: What Changed from FY2023 to FY2024

Every SEC filer's revenue, operating income and net income for two fiscal years, from XBRL. The page breaks the total change down by the SEC review office that covers each company, then by industry, then by company, and ends with a ranked list of where to investigate.

What "variance" means here: not the statistical spread (σ²), but the gap between this year and last year. If revenue was $100M in FY2023 and $120M in FY2024, the variance is +$20M (+20%). Variance analysis explains that gap: which parts of the business added to it, which took away, and which numbers look wrong enough to check.
How to use
  1. Pick a measure: Revenue, Operating income or Net income.
  2. Read the variance bridge from top to bottom: last year's total, what each office added or took away, and this year's total. Click any office bar to break it down by industry, then click an industry to see its companies.
  3. Click a company row anywhere on the page to see both years, the XBRL tags behind each number, and links to the filings.
  4. Where to investigate ranks companies by dollar impact, with the reason each was flagged. Filter it by reason.
