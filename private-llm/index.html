The idea

Most "AI" products are a pipe: your data leaves your device, crosses a network, and gets processed on someone else's servers under someone else's terms. This section collects work that refuses the pipe. Models run locally — Apple Intelligence's on-device model, MLX, llama.cpp, whatever fits — against data that never leaves the machine that produced it.

The interesting engineering isn't just "can a small model run" — it's what becomes possible when the model is allowed to see everything: continuous health streams, voice, calendar, files, all of it, because none of it needs to be shipped anywhere first.

What's in the pipeline

Apple Health stream→ Local model→ Insights on-device
  • HealthKit-connected inference planned — heart rate, sleep, steps, respiratory rate and more already flow into CogniTrust through HealthKit. The private-LLM layer turns that stream into interpretation instead of just storage.
  • Voice watcher intelligence planned — voicelog's always-on transcription tape gets a local model that can actually understand what was said, not just pattern-match keywords.
  • Cognitive assessment interpretation planned — assessment results summarized and contextualized entirely on-device.

Entries land here as pieces ship. Anything that touches a cloud API does not belong in this section.
